Whole Wheat Cardamom Buttermilk Pancakes

Makes twelve (12) pancakes.

Dry Ingredients
  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1½ teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon tartaric acid (cream of tartar)
  • ½ teaspoon cornstarch
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • A few dashes (3 or 4) of ground cardamom
  • ⅛ teaspoon poppy seeds (optional)
Wet Ingredients
  • 1½ cups buttermilk
  • ⅓ cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs

  1. Melt butter in small saucepan over low heat.   Cut butterstick into smaller pieces to speed melting, adding up to ¼ cup hot water if needed.  Remove from heat and let cool slightly.
  2. In medium bowl, whisk eggs and vanilla extract into buttermilk.
  3. Whisk melted butter into buttermilk until well blended.
  4. In large bowl, whisk together flour, cream of tartar, baking soda, cornstarch, salt, cardamom, and poppy seeds.
  5. Make well in center of dry ingredients and pour in wet ingredients.
  6. Gently fold together just until mixed up.  Do not overmix; batter should be slightly lumpy rather than smooth.  If batter is too thick, stir in more milk, a little at a time.
  7. Let batter rest for fifteen (15) minutes.
  8. Toward end of resting, preheat lightly greased skillet over medium heat.  Lightly grease with coconut oil or another high-smoke-point oil.  Pour ladleful of batter onto skillet, using back of ladle to gently spread batter into round shape if needed.
  9. Cook until topside bubbles, edges begin to set, and underside is golden brown.  Flip and cook until golden brown on both sides and cooked all the way through.
  10. Serve warm with maple syrup or fruit jam.
